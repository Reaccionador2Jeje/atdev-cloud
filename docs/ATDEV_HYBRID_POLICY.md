# AtDev Hybrid Policy

**Status:** Adopted economic direction for AtDev Dev  
**Model:** Hybrid  
**Funding:** Voluntary donations + optional professional services

## Purpose

AtDev Dev should remain useful and maintainable even when its available income is zero. Revenue may accelerate development, improve reliability, and pay justified operating costs; it must not become an artificial prerequisite for keeping the open projects accessible.

## 1. Access and pricing

- Free access is the default for core AtDev projects and community features.
- Donations are voluntary and do not unlock essential functionality, priority treatment, or ownership rights.
- Professional services (such as custom development, setup, and support) may be offered separately and optionally.
- Any future paid feature must be clearly identified, must not remove the free core, and must have a documented reason and cost.

## 2. Infrastructure purchasing

- Prefer open-source, portable, and self-hostable technologies.
- Prefer free tiers only when their limits, data portability, and operational constraints are understood.
- No recurring subscription may be started unless the available budget can sustain it without relying on uncertain future donations or sales.
- A paid provider must have a migration or exit plan and a backup/export procedure.
- Provider limits, overage risks, renewal dates, and account ownership must be documented.
- Never expose user data or service secrets to reduce hosting costs.

## 3. Funding sources

### Voluntary donations

- One-time or recurring contributions may be accepted through an appropriate, legally compliant channel.
- Donating is optional and does not determine a user's access to free core services.
- Public reports should avoid exposing donor personal information unless the donor has explicitly agreed.

### Optional professional services

- Custom software development, integrations, deployment assistance, and maintenance may be offered when capacity permits.
- Scope, delivery expectations, and any costs must be agreed before work begins.
- Professional work must not silently convert community features into paid-only features.

## 4. Spending priorities

When funds are available, the suggested order is:

1. Essential hosting and domain renewals.
2. Backups, security, and recovery.
3. Development and testing infrastructure.
4. Accessibility and community improvements.
5. A reserve for future renewals and unexpected costs.

These are planning priorities, not automatic spending commitments. If there is no available budget, do not incur the expense.

## 5. Transparency

- Record confirmed income and expenses in the public finance ledger.
- Mark estimates and planned costs separately from confirmed transactions.
- Report amounts with their original currency; do not combine currencies without a documented exchange rate and date.
- Do not publish passwords, account numbers, payment tokens, private invoices, addresses, or personal donor details.
- Corrections should be made with a dated note rather than silently rewriting historical records.

## 6. Sustainability safeguards

- Maintain an inventory of recurring services, owners, renewal dates, and cancellation steps.
- Review actual usage and free-tier limits before upgrading.
- Set usage alerts or hard application limits where available.
- Keep backups independent of the primary provider where practical.
- Prefer graceful degradation over an unexpected paid dependency.
- Do not promise permanent free hosting where provider quotas or policies can change.

## 7. Governance and review

This policy is the default for AtDev Dev projects. A project may document a justified exception, but it must state the reason, expected cost, funding source, and exit plan. Review the policy at least twice a year and whenever a provider changes its pricing or limits.

## 8. Financial and legal responsibility

Any real-world payment account, donation campaign, contract, tax, or business arrangement must be handled with the appropriate adult/legal oversight and in accordance with applicable local requirements. This repository contains policy and bookkeeping tooling, not payment processing.

---

**AtDev principle:** build freely, spend carefully, disclose honestly, and never make the community carry costs that AtDev cannot sustain.
